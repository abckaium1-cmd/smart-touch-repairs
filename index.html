    FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

    Direct answers regarding our mobile car body repairs, colour matching, appointment scheduling, and service areas.

    Do you come to my home or workplace?

    Yes. Smart Touch operates a fully self-contained mobile repair van that brings all necessary paint mixing tools, spray equipment, and power supplies directly to your driveway or workplace parking space.

    Which areas do you cover?

    We cover Birmingham, Solihull, Sutton Coldfield, Shirley, Dorridge, and surrounding West Midlands areas. Simply enter your postcode in the quote form above to verify service at your exact location.

    What photos should I send for an accurate quote?

    For the most accurate assessment, take two clear photos in natural daylight: one wide shot showing the entire vehicle panel from 2–3 metres back, and one closer shot showing the depth of the scratch, scuff, or dent.

    Can you match my car's exact paint colour?

    Yes. We use a specialist digital spectrum colour-reading device on site to analyse your vehicle's factory paint and formulation, ensuring an accurate and seamless blend with surrounding original bodywork.

    How will you contact me after I request a quote?

    Once you submit your damage details and photos, our technician reviews the damage and contacts you via your preferred method (phone call or text message) with quote details and available appointment slots.

    Does requesting a quote commit me to a booking?

    No. Requesting a quote is completely free of charge and obligation. You decide whether to proceed only after you receive our assessment and price.
